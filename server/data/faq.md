# Brightside Dental: questions callers ask

Each `##` section is one entry in the knowledge base. The optional `keywords:`
line under a heading adds words a caller might use that the text doesn't.
Opening hours, contacts, services, prices, dentists and insurance come from
`clinic.json`; don't repeat them here.

## Parking and getting here

keywords: park, car, bus, directions, find, garage

Free parking in the lot behind the building, entrance from Oak Street. Two accessible spaces are next to the back door. Bus lines 4 and 11 stop at Maple and 3rd, a two-minute walk. We are on the ground floor, Suite 3.

## Wheelchair access

keywords: accessible, disability, wheelchair, stairs, elevator, walker

The office is on the ground floor with step-free access from the parking lot, wide doors and an accessible restroom. Tell us when booking if you need extra time or help.

## First visit: what to bring

keywords: new patient, bring, forms, paperwork, id, card, first time, documents

Bring a photo ID, your insurance card if you have one, and a list of medications you take. We text a link to the new patient forms after booking; filling them in at home saves about 15 minutes. Please arrive 10 minutes early for a first visit.

## Cancelling or rescheduling

keywords: cancel, reschedule, change, move, appointment, missed, fee, no show

Please let us know at least 24 hours ahead, by phone or by replying to the reminder text. Late cancellations and missed appointments are free the first time; after that there is a $50 fee. The receptionist can move an appointment to another free slot during the call.

## Running late

keywords: late, delayed, traffic, minutes

If you will be more than 15 minutes late, please call. We will try to keep the appointment, but we may need to shorten it or move it so the next patient isn't kept waiting.

## Reminders

keywords: reminder, text, sms, email, confirm, confirmation

We send a text and an email when the appointment is booked, and a reminder the day before. Reply C to confirm or R to ask us to reschedule.

## Payment and financing

keywords: pay, card, cash, credit, debit, hsa, fsa, carecredit, financing, payment plan, monthly, apple pay

We take cash, all major credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay, and HSA and FSA cards. Payment is due at the visit. For treatment over $500 we offer monthly payments through CareCredit, interest-free for 6 or 12 months for approved patients.

## No insurance: membership plan

keywords: uninsured, no insurance, without insurance, self pay, membership, discount, savings, cheaper

For patients without insurance, the Brightside membership is $299 a year for adults and $199 for children. It includes two checkups and cleanings, the exams and X-rays, one emergency exam, and 15% off all other treatment. You can join at your first visit.

## Dental emergencies after hours

keywords: night, weekend, after hours, closed, emergency, bleeding, swelling, breathing, fever, er, hospital, 911

During opening hours we see emergencies the same day. After hours, call the main number and choose option 2 to reach the dentist on call. If the face or neck is swelling quickly, there is trouble breathing or swallowing, a high fever, or bleeding that won't stop, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency room straight away.

## Knocked-out tooth

keywords: knocked out, fell out, accident, sports, soccer, football, hockey, playing, fall, injury, lost tooth

Come in straight away; a tooth put back within an hour has the best chance. Hold it by the top, not the root, rinse it briefly without scrubbing and keep it in milk or in the cheek. Call us on the way so we are ready.

## Children

keywords: child, kid, baby, toddler, son, daughter, age, parent, guardian, first visit

We see children from their first tooth or first birthday, whichever comes first, up to age 14, with Dr. Sofia Ramirez. A parent or guardian must come to every visit for patients under 18. Morning appointments usually work best for young children.

## Nervous patients and sedation

keywords: nervous, anxious, anxiety, scared, afraid, fear, phobia, sedation, laughing gas, nitrous, sleep

Tell us when booking and we will allow extra time and go at your pace. Laughing gas (nitrous oxide) is available for most treatments for $75 a visit, and you can drive home afterwards. We don't offer IV sedation or general anesthesia; we refer to a specialist for that.

## Languages

keywords: spanish, español, language, translator, interpreter, speak

Our team speaks English and Spanish; Dr. Okafor and two of the front desk team speak Spanish. For other languages we can book a phone interpreter if you tell us when booking.

## X-rays and safety

keywords: x-ray, radiation, safe, digital, pregnant, pregnancy

We use digital X-rays, which need about 80% less radiation than film. If you are pregnant, tell us: we postpone routine X-rays and most elective treatment, but cleanings are safe and recommended.

## Medical questions and prescriptions

keywords: medicine, prescription, antibiotic, painkiller, advice, medication, refill, side effect, doctor

The front desk can't give medical advice or prescriptions over the phone. A dentist can call you back during opening hours, usually the same day. For an emergency, see dental emergencies after hours.

## Medical records and transfers

keywords: records, x-ray copies, transfer, previous dentist, forms, specialist referral

We can request your records and X-rays from your previous dentist; sign a release form at your first visit. To send your records to another office, email us or ask at the front desk; it takes about two business days.

## Holidays

keywords: holiday, christmas, thanksgiving, new year, july, closed, vacation

We are closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, the 4th of July, Labor Day, Thanksgiving and the day after, and Christmas Eve and Christmas Day. The dentist on call is available for emergencies on these days.
